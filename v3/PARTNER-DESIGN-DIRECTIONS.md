The user rejected decorative gold frames/panels and requested five alternatives, selection of the best one, and removal of all numeric badges. Existing constraints: no black theme; large readable names/locations; authentic textile images; two uninterrupted opposing photo strips; no zoom or pause controls.

1. Editorial gallery - uniform ivory ground, borderless photographs, open captions, asymmetric headline/copy composition. Gold restricted to an accent phrase and short hairlines. Best fit: the varied real photographs and fine cotton texture lead, without simulated luxury decoration.
2. Midnight gallery - uniform navy ground, borderless photography, ivory names and restrained gold type. Strong contrast but too close to the previous direction the user found dull.
3. Two-tone editorial spread - ivory introduction, navy image zone. Clear hierarchy but adds another large color transition between adjacent homepage sections.
4. Textile atelier - warm neutral ground with a faint woven-fabric background. Tactile, but another background texture competes with the already detailed terry photographs and risks the same decorative excess.
5. Typographic lookbook - oversized partner names as the main graphic layer paired with the two image tracks. Memorable but competes with the actual stitched logos and lengthens the section.

Selected and implemented: concept 1. No numerical badges, photo borders, card fills, shadows, repeating background texture, pill labels, or giant background watermark. Photographs retain their square windows and existing 17 assets. Captions use large navy Cormorant Italic names with 14px Montserrat locations. The second track has a negative animation delay to avoid column-like alignment with the first. This does not pause either track or change its seamless duplication logic.

All five above are design concepts, not five rendered website variants. Only the selected concept is implemented. Structural/contrast/marquee checks are available; actual browser rendering could not be verified through the unavailable local browser surface. Root/v2 and all other site sections remain unchanged.
